How the Pool Works

Welcome to the pool! It runs in two rounds across the 2026 World Cup — first you predict how the group stage shakes out, then you fill in the full knockout bracket. Everything is scored on points, and the highest total at the end wins.

💵 Buy-In

$20 to enter — winner takes all. The player with the highest combined point total (group placements + bracket) when the tournament ends takes the entire pot.

🥇 Round 1 — Group Stage Predictions

Before the tournament kicks off, head to the My Picks tab and rank all four teams in each of the 12 groups from 1st to 4th place. You're predicting the final group-stage standings — who wins the group, who finishes runner-up, and who goes home.

🏆 Round 2 — Knockout Bracket

Once the group stage wraps up and the 32 qualifiers are set, the bracket round opens automatically. You'll pick the teams that advance through each knockout round: Round of 32 → Round of 16 → Quarterfinals → Semifinals → Final.

The 32 teams that make the knockout stage are: the top 2 finishers from each of the 12 groups (24 teams), plus the 8 best third-place teams across all groups. The 8 third-place teams are ranked by points, then goal difference, then goals scored — the top 8 of those 12 third-place finishers go through.

📊 Scoring

Each team you place correctly earns the points for its round — so a team you ride all the way to the title earns points at every stage along the way (1 for the R32, 2 for the R16, 4 for the QF, 8 for the SF, and 16 for winning the Final). Your group and bracket points add up to your running total on the Leaderboard.

⚖️ Tiebreaker

When you fill out your bracket in Round 2, you'll also answer one tiebreaker question: how many total goals will be scored across all the knockout rounds? If two or more people finish tied on total points, whoever's guess is closest to the actual number wins.

⏰ Deadlines (all times Eastern, ET / EDT)

  • Round 1 — Group picks lock: Thursday, June 11, 2026 at 3:00 PM ET, right as the opening match (Mexico vs. South Africa) kicks off. Get all 12 groups ranked before then.
  • Round 2 — Bracket picks lock: Sunday, June 28, 2026 at 3:00 PM ET, when the first Round of 32 match kicks off. The bracket round opens once the group stage finishes (June 27), so you'll have a window to fill it out with the real qualified teams.

Note: because the tournament is in June–July, the US is on Eastern Daylight Time. FIFA lists every kickoff in ET, which is what these deadlines follow.

🔐 Your PIN — Editing Your Picks

When you submit your picks you'll set your own 4-digit PIN. This is how you get back in to change anything before the deadline.

  • Write your PIN down. You'll need it every time you want to edit, and there's no "forgot PIN" reset — so don't lose it.
  • Change your mind anytime before lock. Go to My Picks, hit ✏️ Edit next to your name, enter your PIN, and tweak whatever you want. Last version saved before the deadline is the one that counts.
  • Two separate deadlines, two chances to edit. Your group picks are editable up until June 11, and your bracket picks are editable up until June 28 — independently of each other.
  • Once a round locks, it's final. No edits to that round after its deadline, so don't wait until the last minute.
  • Everyone's picks stay hidden until Round 1 locks, so no one can copy off you before the deadline. 🤫
Good luck — may your bracket survive longer than your group-stage darkhorse. ⚽
